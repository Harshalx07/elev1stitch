Questions clients ask

Why is there no single flat price?

Two logos of the same size can need very different work. A one-word logo might be 3,000 stitches; a crest with small lettering can be 15,000. Pricing by size and detail keeps simple jobs cheap and complex jobs done properly.

How fast will I get my file?

Standard delivery is 24 hours after you approve the quote, and rush delivery is 12 hours. Very large or detailed designs can take longer; the delivery time is always written on your quote.

Which formats are included?

All of them, at no extra cost: DST, PES, EMB, EXP, JEF, VP3, DSB, XXX, HUS and more on request. Tell us your machine brand and we send the right one first.

Which machines do your files work on?

Any commercial or home machine that reads standard formats, including Tajima, Barudan, Brother, Ricoma, SWF, Happy, Melco, Janome and Husqvarna.

What should I send for a quote?

Your artwork, the finished width or height, the garment or fabric, and your machine format. A photo of the garment helps for caps, towels and stretch fabric.

Can you work from a photo or a blurry logo?

Yes. We can digitize from a clear photo. If the artwork is too blurry to read, we redraw it as clean vector first, from $10.

Do you sew a test before delivery?

You get a stitch simulation preview with the stitch count and colour sequence to approve before the final file is released. After your first sew-out, tell us how it ran and any small fixes are free.

What counts as a free edit?

Small fixes within 7 days of delivery: density, underlay, pull compensation, stitch direction, colour sequence, or a small text change. Changing the artwork itself or resizing beyond 20% is a new file at 50% of the size price.

Can you fix a file I already have?

Yes. Send the original file and a photo of the sew-out problem. Edits to existing files start at $6.

How do I pay?

All prices and payments are in US dollars through Wise. New clients get a watermarked preview first and pay before the final file is delivered. Regular shops can move to weekly invoicing.

Do you offer bulk or monthly rates?

Yes. Three to five designs in one order get 10% off, six or more get 15% off. Shops sending files every week can ask for a fixed monthly rate.

What if the file doesn't sew well?

Send us a photo of the sew-out and your settings. We fix the file free of charge; if we cannot get it right, you get a refund for that file.

Is my artwork kept private?

Yes. Your artwork, customer names and order details are never shared or resold. We can sign an NDA before you send artwork if you need one.

Can you digitize a logo I don't own?

Only with permission from the owner. By sending artwork you confirm you have the right to have it embroidered.

Terms & conditions

The rules for ordering embroidery digitizing from ELEV1STITCH. Last updated 7 November 2026.

1. About these terms

These terms apply to every order placed with ELEV1STITCH ("we", "us"), an embroidery digitizing studio that delivers files worldwide. By sending artwork for a quote or approving a quote, you ("the client") accept these terms.

2. Quotes and orders

Each quote is based on the artwork, finished size, fabric and stitch style you give us. A quote is valid for 14 days. Work starts only after you approve the quote in writing (email or chat message counts).

If the details change after approval, for example a different size or fabric, we may send a revised quote before continuing.

3. Payment

  • All prices and payments are in US dollars (USD).
  • New clients pay in full before the final machine file is delivered. A watermarked preview is sent first.
  • Payments are accepted through Wise. Transfer fees charged by your bank or provider are paid by the client.
  • Regular clients may be offered weekly or monthly invoicing, payable within 7 days of the invoice date.

4. Turnaround and delivery

Standard turnaround is 24 hours from quote approval; rush orders are 12 hours. Large or highly detailed designs may need longer, and we will tell you the delivery time on the quote. Days are counted in India Standard Time (UTC+05:30).

Files are delivered by email or a download link in the formats you request. You are responsible for saving your files; we keep a copy for 12 months as a courtesy.

5. Revisions and edits

  • Minor edits are free within 7 days of delivery: density, underlay, pull compensation, stitch direction, colour sequence and small text changes.
  • Changes to the artwork, a new size beyond 20% of the original, or a different fabric type are treated as a new file at 50% of the size price.
  • Please send a photo of your sew-out with any edit request so we can see what needs fixing.

6. Sew-out and production results

We digitize every file for the fabric and size you tell us. Final results also depend on your machine, needles, thread, backing, hooping and tension, which are outside our control. Always run a test sew-out on a sample piece before production.

We are not responsible for damaged garments or production runs made before a test sew-out has been checked.

7. Artwork rights

By sending artwork you confirm that you own it or have permission from the owner to have it digitized and embroidered. We do not knowingly digitize trademarks, logos or characters for clients who are not authorised to use them, and we may refuse any order we believe infringes someone else's rights.

8. Ownership of files

Once paid in full, the client may use the delivered machine files for their own production without limit. ELEV1STITCH keeps the right to show the stitched design in its portfolio and social media unless you ask us not to in writing.

9. Confidentiality

Your artwork, customer names and order details are kept private and are never shared with or resold to other clients. If you need a signed non-disclosure agreement, ask before sending artwork.

10. Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel free of charge before we start the file.
  • If you cancel after work has started, 50% of the quoted price is payable.
  • If we cannot fix a problem with a file after reasonable edits, we will refund the price of that file.
  • Delivered and approved files cannot be returned.

11. Limit of liability

Our total liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for that order. We are not liable for indirect losses such as lost profits, garment costs, machine time or missed deadlines of your own customers.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute that cannot be settled by discussion falls under the courts of India.

13. Privacy

When you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address, any shop details you add, your message and your artwork. We use this only to answer you, quote and deliver your order.

  • The website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party trackers or fonts.
  • Form messages are delivered straight to our business mailbox and are not stored on the website.

  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by emailing us.
